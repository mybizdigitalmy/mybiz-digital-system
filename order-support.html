Questions about an NFC Smart Stand order?

ORDER SUPPORT

Keep your order reference handy.

When contacting us, include the order reference (if one has been issued), business name, product type and the support issue. You should not share passwords, one-time passcodes or full payment-card details.

What can we help with?

01

Order and payment enquiries

Ask about an order, payment confirmation or purchase document. Live online payment processing and automated order-history features remain pending launch and verification.

02

Order amendments

Request a change to the delivery address, quantity, destination link or business information as soon as possible. Changes are reviewed according to programming and dispatch progress and are not guaranteed.

03

Delivery enquiries

Our selected delivery providers are Pos Laju within Malaysia and DHL for international shipments. Delivery times, charges and tracking information depend on the confirmed shipment and destination.

04

Damage, 5-year chip warranty and replacements

Report damaged, defective or incorrect items with supporting photographs. The five-year NFC chip warranty covers verified chip defects under normal intended use from confirmed delivery. We review claims and arrange the appropriate remedy according to the issue and applicable policies.

How support requests are handled

Before dispatch

Contact us promptly to request an amendment or cancellation. We will confirm whether NFC programming or shipment preparation has begun and advise on the available options, including any price difference.

After dispatch

Shipment changes may not be possible once the courier has received the parcel. If a customer-supplied destination needs to change after dispatch, a newly programmed replacement may be ordered with a 10% discount for eligible returning customers; delivery is charged separately.

Item arrives damaged

Report the damage promptly. Send the product photographs, packaging images and shipping label with your order reference. If the damage is verified as arising before or during delivery, MyBizDigital arranges a replacement free of charge. A physical return is not always required.

Chip malfunction or wrong programming

We assess NFC issues and verify the original programmed destination. Verified errors made by MyBizDigital are remedied without charge, including a newly programmed replacement where necessary. Verified eligible chip defects are assessed under our five-year NFC chip warranty.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change my order after placing it?

Please contact us before dispatch. We will check whether the change is possible based on the NFC configuration stage and courier handover status. A change affecting quantity or shipping may require an adjusted amount.

Will I need to return a badly damaged stand?

Not necessarily. We may approve a replacement without return when clear evidence is sufficient. Please retain the damaged item and packaging until we confirm the resolution.

Who pays when an item arrives damaged?

When our review confirms a MyBizDigital-caused or transit-damaged delivery, MyBizDigital arranges the replacement and replacement delivery without charging the customer.

How will tracking work after online checkout launches?

After the operational order-management system is launched and verified, customers are intended to receive order and shipment emails and see relevant progress in their customer account. Until then, please contact support for any existing enquiry or arrangement.

Can I order just one Food Menu NFC Smart Stand?

No. The Food Menu offer is a bundle of 10 preconfigured stands for RM800, not an individually sold product. Contact us if you need to discuss the destination link or intended menu setup.

What about international orders?

DHL is the selected international courier. Delivery availability, charges, customs responsibilities and international warranty shipping arrangements must be confirmed for the destination before an order is accepted.
