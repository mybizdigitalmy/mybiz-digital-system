Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using NFC Smart Stands in your business.

What is an NFC Smart Stand, and how does it work?
An NFC Smart Stand has a small contactless tag programmed with your business link. A customer holds a compatible phone close to the stand, and the phone can open your Google review page, social media profile, or digital menu. The customer may need to confirm the link on their phone.
Does it work with iPhone and Android? Do customers need an app?
Yes, it works with compatible NFC-enabled iPhones and Android phones. Customers do not need a separate MyBizDigital app or camera scan. They may need to unlock their phone, enable NFC where required, and tap an on-screen notification. Internet access is needed to load the linked page.
Does the Google Review NFC Stand automatically write or submit a review?
No. It opens the business’s Google review destination, making it easier for customers to find the form. Customers sign in to Google if needed, choose their own star rating, write honest feedback, and press Post themselves. A tap never creates or guarantees a review.
Will the Social Media NFC Stand automatically make customers follow us?
No. It opens the linked Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok profile or page. The customer can choose whether to tap Follow or Like, depending on the platform. They may need to sign in to that service first.
Can customers order food directly through the Food Menu NFC Stand?
The stand opens your digital food menu. If your linked menu includes an ordering system that has been set up for your restaurant, customers can add dishes, review their basket, and place an order. If you use a menu-only link, customers can browse the menu but cannot submit an order through the stand alone.
Does the NFC Smart Stand need batteries, charging, or Wi-Fi?
No. The NFC tag inside the stand is passive, so the stand itself needs no battery, charging, or Wi-Fi connection. Customers use their compatible phone to read the tag, and their phone needs an internet connection to open the linked website or app.
