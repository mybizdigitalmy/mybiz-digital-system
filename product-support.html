PRODUCT SUPPORT

Your NFC stand is ready. We're here if you need help.

MyBizDigital configures your NFC Smart Stand before delivery. Place it where customers can conveniently reach it—there is no chip programming required on your side. If something is not working, this page explains what to check and how to get assistance.

READY TO USE

We configure it. You place it. Your customers tap.

Your programmed stand opens its intended Google review, social-media or food-menu destination when tapped with a compatible smartphone. Visitors decide what to do on the destination page; a tap never automatically posts a review or follows a social account.

Having trouble with a tap?

01

Check the phone

Confirm the device supports NFC. Some Android phones require NFC to be enabled in Settings. NFC reading behaviour varies across devices.

02

Try a different tap position

Hold the phone near the NFC area of the stand for a moment. The antenna position can vary between smartphones. Thick cases or metal attachments may interfere.

03

Try another compatible device

If possible, test the same stand with another NFC-capable smartphone. This helps determine whether the issue is with a particular phone or the stand.

04

Contact support

If neither device opens the intended link, report the issue and include your order reference, product type and a short photo or video demonstrating the problem.

Replacement and technical assistance

5-Year NFC Chip Warranty

Our five-year chip warranty covers verified NFC chip failure under normal intended use, starting from confirmed delivery. Approved eligible chip-defect replacements are provided without charge, including replacement delivery within Malaysia. Contact MyBizDigital with your order reference for troubleshooting and assessment; additional evidence or inspection may be required. Accidental damage and customer-requested destination changes are not chip defects. International replacement shipping is assessed case by case.

Damaged when delivered

Report the issue promptly with photographs of the stand, outer packaging and shipping label. For verified delivery-related or MyBizDigital-caused damage, we arrange a replacement without charging you. For severe damage, we may approve a replacement without asking you to return the product.

Business link changed after delivery

Our stands are configured before delivery; remote rewriting is not offered. If the supplied destination changes after dispatch, request a newly programmed replacement. Eligible returning customers receive a 10% replacement-product discount, excluding delivery charges.

Accidental damage after delivery

Send your original order reference and photographs. Accidental damage is assessed separately from chip defects; eligible returning customers may purchase a replacement stand with a 10% product discount.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to program my NFC stand?

No. MyBizDigital programs and tests the supplied destination before dispatch. Simply place your stand where customers can tap it.

Will tapping a Google Review stand automatically submit a review?

No. The tap opens the intended Google review destination where available. The visitor chooses their own rating, writes feedback if they wish and submits it themselves.

Can MyBizDigital change the NFC destination remotely?

No. Remote destination rewriting is not currently offered. Contact us about an eligible newly programmed replacement if your business link changes.

How long is the NFC chip warranty?

Five years from confirmed delivery for verified NFC chip defects under normal intended use. It is not a five-year warranty against accidental damage to the whole stand. Coverage is subject to the published warranty policy and applicable law.

What should I send for a damage or warranty request?

Provide the order reference, business name, product type, a clear description of the issue and photographs or a short video. For delivery damage, include the packaging and delivery label if available.

Need product assistance?

Contact MyBizDigital with your order reference and the issue you are experiencing. We will review the information and advise you on the next steps.

Support: Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM (Malaysia time). Closed Sunday.
